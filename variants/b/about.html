About Collect & Connect

Collect & Connect buys, sells, and trades video games and collectibles.

We're a downtown Fostoria storefront built around the joy of the hunt — retro and newer video games, action figures, trading cards, comics, Pokemon, Magic: The Gathering, vintage toys, posters, local items, and more.

You'll find us at 115 W. Center St., next to the barber shop and antique store, with display cases full of things worth a closer look.
